Process

How I Direct & Ship High-Impact Motion

A refined 4-stage pipeline taking raw concepts into polished 4K cinematic animations.

01

Creative Direction, Moodboards & Styleframes

Before animating, I establish the visual grammar: moodboards, cinematic lighting references, color scripts, and high-fidelity styleframes in Photoshop/Cinema 4D to align on creative vision.
02

3D World-Building & Look Development

Sculpting, procedural geometry nodes, and asset lookdev. Applying photorealistic PBR materials, custom procedural textures, and cinematic volumetric lighting rigs.
03

Fluid Keyframing, Dynamics & Camera Choreography

Breathing life into the scene with mathematical easing curves, dynamic camera lenses, cloth/particle simulations, and rhythmic audio synchronization.
04

4K Octane/Redshift Rendering & Master Color Grade

Multi-pass AOVs rendered via GPU engines (Redshift/Octane), composited in After Effects with optical lens flares and grain, and master color graded in DaVinci Resolve.
